Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

WorkHub collects personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and billing details to facilitate desk reservations and membership subscriptions across our co-working hubs.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to process bookings, provide digital access keys, issue payment receipts, send operational notifications, and enhance our workspace amenities.

3. Data Security & Encryption

All sensitive transactions are processed using 256-bit SSL encryption and PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We never store raw payment card details on our servers.

4. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to your stored personal data, edit your profile settings, or request account deletion at any time by contacting support@workhub.io.
