Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan? ▲
You can change your plan at any time through the "My Membership" dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately with pro-rated billing, while downgrades occur at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a minimum commitment period? ▼
No! All our memberships operate on a flexible month-to-month model. You can cancel or pause your subscription anytime with zero penalty fees.
Can I bring guests to the workspace? ▼
Yes, Professional and Business plan members receive guest passes each month. Additional guest day passes can be purchased via your profile dashboard.
Do you offer student or non-profit discounts? ▼
We offer a 15% discount for verified full-time students, academic researchers, and registered non-profit organization staff. Contact support for verification.
